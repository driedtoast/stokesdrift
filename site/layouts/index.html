Frequently asked questions

How does stokesdrift track equipment?

stokesdrift generates a unique QR code for each item you register. When you scan that QR code with the mobile app, it automatically records the item's GPS location and timestamp. You can also manually set an address for items being shipped remotely.

What is a checkout scan?

A checkout scan compares the items you scanned at load-in against the items you scan at load-out. It instantly shows you anything missing so you never leave gear behind at a venue. It's the most valuable feature for touring bands and event crews.

Does stokesdrift work without internet?

Yes. stokesdrift is offline-first. All scans and item changes are saved to a local database on your phone. When you reconnect, the app syncs automatically using conflict-free CRDT technology. This means multiple team members can scan independently and everything merges cleanly.

How much does stokesdrift cost?

stokesdrift is free for solo use with up to 100 items. The Crew plan costs $9/month for up to 5 team members with unlimited items. The Enterprise plan costs $29/month for unlimited members, custom fields, and API access.

Who is stokesdrift for?

stokesdrift is built for touring bands, event production crews, AV teams, photo and video production, and anyone managing gear that moves between locations. It's designed for people who need to know where their stuff is right now — not just what they own.

What makes stokesdrift different from other asset trackers?

Most asset trackers are built for warehouses and stationary inventory. stokesdrift is built for things that move. The checkout scan catches missing items before you leave, the offline-first design works at festivals and remote venues with no cell service, and GPS breadcrumbs show the full journey of each item.
